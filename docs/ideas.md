# Ideas

Ideas for later. None of these are planned yet.

## Endgame

- **Star dungeons:** from star 1, a rare 6-star "Mythic" dungeon appears on the
  map, with tougher rooms and champions and a star-only prize (cosmetic or a
  title, not raw power).
- **Reforging:** spend weapon parts or armor scrap to reroll a piece of gear's
  bonus effects. Gives spare parts a use and keeps a better roll to chase.
- **Weekly champion:** one named boss each week with a scoring rule (fewest
  turns, no healing, a single monster). You compete with your own best for a
  badge.
- **Collection goals:** Journal pages for shinies and perfect-gene monsters,
  with titles for completing them.

## Pacing

- **Spell rank and champion limits** are in (3.13). If heroes still max out too
  fast, slow the late XP curve or tie star ranks to a longer climb.
