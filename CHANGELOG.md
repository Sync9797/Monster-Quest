# Changelog

## 1.13.0

- **Weapon class bonus:** a weapon of your class adds +2, +4, +6 or +8
  (Normal, Rare, Epic, Legendary) to Attack, Magic, Defense and Speed, on
  top of the +20% power it already had. A weapon outside your class takes
  the same amount off. The penalty fades as you fight with that weapon and
  is gone after 10 battles. Practice counts for that one weapon only: a
  Warrior who masters one staff starts over with the next.
- **Subclasses at level 15:** three for each class.

  | Class | Subclasses |
  |---|---|
  | Warrior | Berserker (more Attack, stronger as HP drops), Paladin (Defense, better healing, starts behind a ward), Warlord (your monsters +8%) |
  | Mage | Elementalist (+25% spell damage), Cleric (+50% healing and wards), Warlock (spells drain life) |
  | Rogue | Assassin (more and bigger critical hits), Trickster (dodge, feints that throw foes off), Beastmaster (taming odds, monsters +5%) |
  | Adventurer | Jack of All Trades (every weapon counts as yours), Survivor (more HP, heals every turn), Wayfarer (+20% gold and XP) |

  The first pick is free; switching costs 300 gold.
- **Hero skills:** a skill slot opens at levels 30, 45 and 60. Skills rank
  up every 10 levels (up to rank 5), and from rank 3 some cover your whole
  team. Pick from Morale Master, Kleptomaniac (carry a second weapon),
  Insomnia, Intimidate, Hover, Oblivious, Quick Step, Treasure Hunter,
  Scholar, Second Wind, First Strike, Beast Whisperer, Regeneration, Eagle
  Eye and Mana Well. Swapping a skill costs 500 gold.
- **Monster skills at level 30:** every monster gets one that fits what it
  is. Birds, bats, wisps and fairies levitate (Earth moves miss them; Earth
  monsters never do), dragons intimidate, hounds lead their pack, slimes
  and trees regenerate, and shells and bears have thick hides. Wild
  monsters have them too.
- **Selling:** the shop now buys items at half price, and weapons and armor
  by rarity and level. Smelt a weapon for 50 gold to get 1 to 4 parts of
  its type back, depending on rarity.

## 1.12.1

- **Battles on a half-open fold:** with the phone folded to about 90°
  (tabletop), the moves used to sit below the bottom edge and needed
  scrolling. The lower half now shows your team on the left and the moves
  on the right, each scrolling on its own if needed.

## 1.12.0

- **New type chart:** Electric now beats Flight, and Flight beats Earth. It's
  still perfectly balanced, like rock-paper-scissors with seven throws: every
  type is strong against two, weak to two and neutral to two.

  | Type | Strong against | Weak to |
  |---|---|---|
  | Water | Fire, Gas | Earth, Electric |
  | Fire | Earth, Electric | Water, Corrupt |
  | Corrupt | Fire, Flight | Earth, Gas |
  | Gas | Corrupt, Electric | Water, Flight |
  | Earth | Water, Corrupt | Fire, Flight |
  | Flight | Earth, Gas | Electric, Corrupt |
  | Electric | Water, Flight | Fire, Gas |

  The Type chart on the Monsters tab and the Help screen show the new chart.

## 1.11.1

- **Stronger mythics:** Prism Fairy and Mimic are now as strong as a final
  evolution (stats x1.45, stronger signature attacks), still below the
  starters and legendaries. To match, they're rarer: Epic rarity (harder to
  find and to tame) and found from level 15. Ones you already own get the
  boost too.

## 1.11.0

- **Tasks:** things without a due date now have their own **Tasks** section
  at the end of the Quests list (shown only when you have some), separate
  from the **Scheduled** quests grouped by when they're due. Add one with the
  new **+ Task** button; tap a task to give it a due date later. **+ To-do**
  now starts with a due time this evening, which you can change.
- **Starter signature moves:** each starter line learns two strong moves no
  other monster has, at level 22 and level 40:
  - Voltfawn line: **Storm Antlers** (hits every foe, 30% shock each) and
    **Heaven's Charge** (130 power, pierces guards and raised Defense, raises
    Speed).
  - Cindrake line: **Wyrmfire** (always burns, plus 3 turns of scorching) and
    **Inferno Wings** (hits every foe, then raises Attack).
  - Drizzlet line: **Tidal Embrace** (heals half the damage it deals) and
    **Maelstrom Vortex** (115 power, brings rain, traps the foe for 4 turns).
  - Mosscub line: **Grove Guardian** (heals the whole team 20% and raises
    everyone's Defense) and **Tectonic Fury** (hits every foe, 30% chance
    each loses its turn).

## 1.2.0

- **Fixed: folding or unfolding the phone crashed the app.** The map tore
  itself down every time it moved between the folded and unfolded layouts;
  it now carries over intact. The app also handles more display changes
  itself instead of restarting.
- **More monsters**, especially while walking: more spawn in the wild (and
  many more while you're moving), and a monster follows your footsteps every
  600 steps (was 1,000), with up to 4 waiting beside you.
- **Monsters gather around shrines:** most shrines have a monster or two
  nearby (boss shrines more), favoring types that suit the place: Water by
  fountains, Earth in parks, Fire at cafes, Electric at shops and so on. They
  stay by their shrine and never ambush.
- **Dens:** rocky caves on the map, each holding one strong ordinary monster
  (never a legendary): a few levels above you, more HP, no stat below 10 and
  two perfect stats. No loot, just the monster. It can't be tamed mid-fight:
  beat it, then you get 5 to 10 throws to tame it, each one a long shot
  (about half of den monsters end up tamed). Dens move every day and show in
  the Nearby list too.
- **Taming always pays XP:** taming now gives more hero XP than beating a
  monster, and taming a den monster gives double.
- **One dagger:** the hero holds a single dagger in the hand (daggers still
  strike twice).

## 1.0.0

- **Real places on the map:** tap a shrine to see what it really is. Shrines
  get themed names by the kind of place (Supply Cache at shops, Provision
  Shrine at cafes and restaurants, Lore Shrine at libraries, Grove Shrine at
  parks, Muse Stone at public art, Spring Shrine at fountains, Sanctum at
  civic buildings) with the real place and street underneath, like
  "Supply Cache / Barnes & Noble on Wards Road". Bookstores, supermarkets,
  bakeries, game and toy stores and other shops are now shrines too. When the
  map data has no street, the phone looks it up. Wild monsters, tracked
  monsters and villain camps say what they're near and which street they're on.
- **Walk here:** every shrine, monster and villain you're not in range of has
  a Walk here button that opens walking directions in your map app (Google
  Maps, or whichever map app you use).
- **Hero magic:** the hero no longer borrows monster spells. Heroes study
  monster magic and copy it: simpler spells that start at 75% strength and
  reach full strength by level 25.
  - One basic bolt for each type: Ember, Spark, Tide, Gust, Venom, Shadow and
    Stone Bolt.
  - **Mend** heals one ally or yourself. **Ward** puts a shield on one ally or
    yourself that soaks up damage until it breaks. **Fortify** raises one
    ally's Defense.
  - Every spell ranks up to 5 with spell pages and gold (Hero tab), adding
    power (Fortify gives +2 Defense at rank 3 and +3 at rank 5). Pages now
    also drop for spells you already know.
  - Ember Bolt, Mend and Ward are known from the start. Old spells carry over
    (Inferno and Mega Heal become rank 2 Ember Bolt and Mend), and saved pages
    move to their new spell.

## 0.2.1

- **Battery saver** (Saver button on the map): the screen goes black at
  minimum brightness while the game keeps counting your walk and watching for
  monsters. Press and hold anywhere to unlock.
- **The phone vibrates when a new monster appears** within 200 m, on the map
  or the battery saver screen. Turn it off in Settings.

## 0.2.0

The "Polish" update.

- **Settings** (gear left of your level):
  - Step sync from the phone's step counter, Samsung Health, Health Connect
    (Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin and more) or GPS distance.
  - Calendar options: auto sync, look-ahead, all-day events, clear calendar quests.
  - Performance: reduced visual effects and battery saver GPS.
- **Map:** shrines are tall beacons with floating crystals and beams of light;
  monsters stand on the map; villain camps fly banners; your hero stands in a
  gold ring.
- **Candy:** taming gives 3 candy for that monster's line and releasing gives
  more; spend candy to level monsters up. Stat points never change with level.
- **Storage:** 20 monster slots to start; level upgrades grow the bag and
  monster storage together; buy +50 for 1,000 gold.
- **15 new monsters:** slime, rock golem, thunderbird, shell turtle and imp lines.
- **Mythic art style:** ink outlines, engraving, rune circles, mist and glowing
  eyes; evolutions gain auras, spirit wings, halos and crowns.
- **Journal, armor, gear on the hero, Pokémon GO-style spawns and lures,
  Adventurer class, quests dashboard,** thugs and villains, weapon types and
  rarities, and walking rewards.
- New original app icon and taming-orb design.
