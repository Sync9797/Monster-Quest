  - Emberkit (kitten) → Cinderfang (sabre cat) → Pyremane (great lion) →
    Sun Manticore (winged lion).
  - Zapmouse (mouse) → Voltrat (giant rat) → Thunderhog (quill-hog) → Storm
    Behemoth (tusked boar).
  - Toxbug (bug) → Mirepede (centipede) → Stingscor (scorpion) → Plague Mantis.
  - Gloomwisp (wisp) → Gloomshade (ghost) → Nightwraith (wraith) → Abyss Lord
    (a shadow god with a great eye).
  - Pebblit (pebble) → Boulderon (rock golem) → Magma Colossus (titan).
  - Shellsnap (turtle) → Coralback (snapping turtle) → Bastion Tortoise.
  - Ember Pup → Cinder Warg → Inferno Cerberus (the other pups still become

## 1.3.0

- **Evolutions are new creatures.** Second, third and fourth forms no longer
  look like a bigger version of the first: every evolved form is its own
  fierce creature, with burning eyes, fangs, claws, horns and wings in its
  type's colors. For example:
  - Emberkit → Cinderfang (a sabre-toothed cat) → Pyre Cerberus (three
    heads) → Sun Manticore.
  - Zapmouse → Voltquill (a quill beast) → Stormbrute → Raijin Dragon.
  - Bubblefin → Fangfin (a shark) → Reefjaw (an anglerfish) → Leviathan.
  - Gloomwisp → Dreadwing (a giant bat) → Nightstalker (a spider) → Abyss Lord
    (a wraith).
  - Hound pups grow into Wargs, then armored, horned Fenrirs.
  - Starters: Stormstag is a kirin, Infernodrake a wyvern, Maelstrom Serpent
    an eastern dragon, Terraursa a stone titan.
- **New names** for evolved forms that became different creatures (Blazecat
  is now Cinderfang, Skylark is Razorwing, Gloombat is Dreadwing, Cinder Hound
  is Cinder Warg, and so on). Your monsters keep their nicknames.
- **Stronger evolutions:** each evolved form's base stats went up, to x1.4 /
  x1.85 / x2.35 of the first form in four-stage lines (was x1.3 / x1.65 / x2)
  and x1.5 / x2 in three-stage lines (was x1.35 / x1.7). Starters are x1.5 /
  x2.05: still the strongest lines short of the legendaries. Stat points
  (IVs) are unchanged.

## 1.2.0

- **Fixed: folding or unfolding the phone crashed the app.** The map tore
  itself down every time it moved between the folded and unfolded layouts;
  it now carries over intact. The app also handles more display changes
  itself instead of restarting.
- **More monsters**, especially while walking: more spawn in the wild (and
  many more while you're moving), and a monster follows your footsteps every
  600 steps (was 1,000), with up to 4 waiting beside you.
- **Monsters gather around shrines:** most shrines have a monster or two
  nearby (boss shrines more), favoring types that suit the place: Water by
  fountains, Earth in parks, Fire at cafes, Electric at shops and so on. They
  stay by their shrine and never ambush.
- **Dens:** rocky caves on the map, each holding one strong ordinary monster
  (never a legendary): a few levels above you, more HP, no stat below 10 and
  two perfect stats. No loot, just the monster. It can't be tamed mid-fight:
  beat it, then you get 5 to 10 throws to tame it, each one a long shot
  (about half of den monsters end up tamed). Dens move every day and show in
  the Nearby list too.
- **Taming always pays XP:** taming now gives more hero XP than beating a
  monster, and taming a den monster gives double.
- **One dagger:** the hero holds a single dagger in the hand (daggers still
  strike twice).

## 1.1.0

- **New armor pieces:** hoods, cloaks, pants and boots, worn alongside body
  armor (one piece per slot). Each has its own buffs:
  - Hoods: Magic and a little dodge (suit Mages and Rogues).
  - Cloaks: harder to hit (suit Rogues and Adventurers).
  - Pants: Defense and some Attack (suit Warriors and Adventurers).
  - Boots: +12% Speed (suit everyone).
  They use the same rarities as all gear. Rare and better pieces always come
  with a signature buff (Focused +10% Magic, Swift +10% Speed, Mighty +10%
  Attack...), plus more random buffs the rarer they are. Your hero wears them
  too: a trimmed hood, a colored cloak, pants with knee guards and boots.
- **Distances in feet and miles** (or meters and kilometers in Settings).
- **Nearby list:** only shows things within 10 miles. Tapping one moves the
  map there and shows a card with **Walk here** (directions) and **Details**.
  Monsters get a temporary red pin so you can see where to go. Farther
  monsters from the tracker are listed here too.
- **Big screens and unfolded phones** no longer show the tracker bar over the
  map, since the Nearby list beside it already lists those monsters.
- **Fixed:** the hero now holds daggers by the grip, not the blade.

## 1.0.1

- Tapping the map inside the purple reach circle no longer pops up an empty
  white bubble.

## 1.0.0

- **Real places on the map:** tap a shrine to see what it really is. Shrines
  get themed names by the kind of place (Supply Cache at shops, Provision
  Shrine at cafes and restaurants, Lore Shrine at libraries, Grove Shrine at
  parks, Muse Stone at public art, Spring Shrine at fountains, Sanctum at
  civic buildings) with the real place and street underneath, like
  "Supply Cache / Barnes & Noble on Wards Road". Bookstores, supermarkets,
  bakeries, game and toy stores and other shops are now shrines too. When the
  map data has no street, the phone looks it up. Wild monsters, tracked
  monsters and villain camps say what they're near and which street they're on.
- **Walk here:** every shrine, monster and villain you're not in range of has
  a Walk here button that opens walking directions in your map app (Google
  Maps, or whichever map app you use).
- **Hero magic:** the hero no longer borrows monster spells. Heroes study
  monster magic and copy it: simpler spells that start at 75% strength and
  reach full strength by level 25.
  - One basic bolt for each type: Ember, Spark, Tide, Gust, Venom, Shadow and
    Stone Bolt.
  - **Mend** heals one ally or yourself. **Ward** puts a shield on one ally or
    yourself that soaks up damage until it breaks. **Fortify** raises one
    ally's Defense.
  - Every spell ranks up to 5 with spell pages and gold (Hero tab), adding
    power (Fortify gives +2 Defense at rank 3 and +3 at rank 5). Pages now
    also drop for spells you already know.
  - Ember Bolt, Mend and Ward are known from the start. Old spells carry over
    (Inferno and Mega Heal become rank 2 Ember Bolt and Mend), and saved pages
    move to their new spell.

## 0.5.5

- **Tracker distances:** each monster in the tracker bar shows how far away it
  is (feet up close, then yards, then miles) and its direction.
- **Tap a tracked monster for details:** distance and direction, the landmark
  it's near (a real cafe, park, museum... or a shrine), whether it's closing
  in, and when it leaves. "Show area" pans the map toward it.
- **Units setting:** feet and yards (default) or meters, in Settings, then
  General. Distances everywhere on the map follow it.

## 0.5.4

- **Choose which calendars to sync:** Settings, then Calendar, lists every
  calendar on your phone (grouped by account, with its color). Tick only the
  ones you want as quests, or keep "All calendars". Changes sync right away,
  and unfinished quests from calendars you untick are removed. Holiday and
  birthday calendars can now be picked on purpose.

## 0.5.3

- **Earth legendary: Worldroot Colossus**, a stone giant with a forest growing
  from its shoulders and an amber heart. It guards Gold shrines alongside the
  other legendaries (every type now has one) and can be tamed after you
  defeat it once. 84 monsters in all.

## 0.5.2

- **New Earth type** (stone and grass). To keep types balanced there are now
  seven, and every type is strong against two and weak to two (see the Type
  chart). Earth beats Flight and Water, and is weak to Fire and Gas. Classic
  pairs stay (Electric > Water > Fire > Corrupt, Gas > Electric); Flight no
  longer beats Gas.
- **Earth moves:** Seed Bomb (40), Mulch (heals 10%), Sap (40, heals 10%),
  Rock Throw (60), Mega Sap (60, heals 25%), Vine Whip (80), Earthquake (100)
  and Leaf Storm (120, must rest next turn).
- **Earth monsters:** a fourth starter, #10 Mosscub > Grovebear > Terraursa
  (pseudo-legendary); the Twiglet tree line (Twiglet > Barkwarden > Ancient
  Treant); and the Moss hound breed. 83 monsters in all.
- Earth monsters are a little more common at dawn.

## 0.5.1

- **Existing players get a new starter too:** if you started before 0.5, the
  game offers you a free pick of Voltfawn, Cindrake or Drizzlet the next time
  you open it. It joins near your level (5 to 15), with 3 candy, and goes into
  your party if there's room. "Decide later" asks again next time.

## 0.5.0

The "Legends" update.

- **New starters #1, #4 and #7:** Voltfawn (Electric stag), Cindrake (Fire
  drake) and Drizzlet (Water serpent). Each evolves twice (at 16 and 36) into
  a pseudo-legendary: Thunderhorn, Infernodrake and Maelstrom Serpent, stronger
  than every other monster except the legendaries. New games choose one of
  them; they're also very rare in the wild.
- **Shinies:** about 1 in 200 wild monsters (1 in 100 with a lure) is shiny,
  with rare colors and sparkles. Shinies show on the map, in battle and on
  their profile, buzz when in range, and are tracked in the Journal.
- **Legendaries are the strongest monsters to own:** their HP, Attack and
  Defense are now higher than any other monster's. As Gold shrine guardians
  they fight exactly as before.
- 74 monsters in all.

## 0.4.2

- **Resting heals everyone:** each hour the hero and every monster (party,
  bench and away on adventures) heal 5% of their max HP, about a full heal in
  20 hours.
- **Knocked-out monsters wake up on their own** once they rest back to 10% HP
  (about 2 hours); their profile shows how long. Restore Potions still bring
  one back instantly at full HP.

## 0.4.1

- **Back goes back:** the Back button returns to the previous screen instead
  of closing the app (a monster's profile returns to the list, Journal and
  Adventures return to the collection, Help returns to Settings, Settings and
  other tabs return to Quests). In battle, Back closes sub-menus and never
  quits a fight; during a phone-free session it can't fail you by accident.
- **Drag and drop your team:** long-press a monster and drag it onto a battle
  party slot. Dropping on a filled slot swaps them; drag a party member out to
  bench it.

## 0.4.0

The "Hunt" update.

- **Monsters come to you:** new monsters appear out in a ring 300-800 m away
  and creep toward you over time. They hear you walking: while you move they
  close in much faster. Curious ones reach you even if you stand still.
- **Tracker bar** on the map (like Pokémon GO's "Nearby"): monsters you can
  sense but not yet see, with footprints for distance and the direction to
  walk. Only monsters within about 200 m stand on the map.
- **Walking reward:** every 1,000 steps a monster follows your footsteps, even
  with the phone in your pocket. Up to 3 wait beside you on the map.
- **Packs:** nearby monsters may team up (2 vs 4, 3 vs 3, 4 vs 4...). Tap a pack
  member to target it. Packs are tougher and pay up to 2.1x more. Not every
  monster joins in.
- **Ambushes:** aggressive monsters (more at night) that reach you attack
  first, with a hard buzz. At most one every 10 minutes; can be turned off.
- **Vibration** when a monster comes within battle range.
- **Help** in Settings: how quests, phone-free sessions, walking, the map,
  collecting items, battles, taming, boss raids and villains work.

## 0.3.2

- **120 fps:** the game asks for your screen's highest refresh rate (up to
  120 Hz). Settings, then Performance, then Frame rate: 120 fps (default),
  60 fps to save battery, or Auto. The battery saver screen drops to 60.
- **Loot pop-up:** collecting from a shrine shows what you got, with icons,
  in a short pop-up that fades away on its own (tap to close).
- **Smoother screens:** monster, hero and icon art is drawn once and reused,
  so long lists and battles scroll and animate smoothly.
- **Cleanup:** removed unused icons, helpers and imports left over from
  earlier versions.

## 0.3.1

- **Rewards follow the monster's strength.** Wild monsters already scale with
  your team; now XP, gold and monster XP do too: x0.6 alone, x1.25 with 2,
  x2 with 3 and x2.75 with a full team of 4. Going in alone is easier but pays
  less; a 4-on-1 is a harder fight for much more.
- **Better drops from bigger fights:** more frequent item drops, plus a bonus
  drop for teams of 3 or 4 (Hi-Potions, Great Orbs, Phoenix Downs, and a small
  chance of a Restore Potion).
- The battle screen shows the reward multiplier next to the enemy.

## 0.3.0

The "Battle" update.

- **Battle animations:** spells and typed moves throw a bolt of their type
  (fireballs, lightning, water orbs, wind crescents, gas clouds, shadow orbs);
  sword attacks and Normal moves slash. Targets shake and flash with floating
  damage numbers (gold for critical and super-effective hits); misses and
  heals show too.
- **Clear turns:** the attacker gets a gold ring, the fighter whose turn it is
  pulses with "Your move", and a turn order strip shows who acts next.
- **Tougher wild monsters that scale with your team:** the more fighters you
  bring (hero plus up to 3 monsters), the more HP, Attack and Defense a wild
  monster has; a full team of 4 faces about 4x HP and 2x Attack, a lone hero
  faces a weaker one. Bigger fights pay up to 45% more XP and gold.
- **Bosses and villains** keep their strength against a full team and get
  easier when you bring fewer fighters.

  down for a moment and the screen goes dark; turn it upright to return

## 0.2.3

- **Phone calls never fail a phone-free session.** Answering or making a call
  (including calls in apps like WhatsApp or Teams) doesn't count as leaving
  the game. After you hang up you have 15 seconds to come back.

## 0.2.2

- **Phone-free sessions keep the screen on.** The timer stays up and dims
  itself after 15 seconds (tap to brighten), so the phone never locks and
  unlocking can't fail you. The session only fails if you leave Monster Quest
  for another app and don't come back within 15 seconds. Turning the screen
  off with the power button is fine.

## 0.2.1

- **Battery saver** (Saver button on the map): the screen goes black at
  minimum brightness while the game keeps counting your walk and watching for
  monsters. Press and hold anywhere to unlock.
- **The phone vibrates when a new monster appears** within 200 m, on the map
  or the battery saver screen. Turn it off in Settings.

## 0.2.0

The "Polish" update.

- **Settings** (gear left of your level):
  - Step sync from the phone's step counter, Samsung Health, Health Connect
    (Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin and more) or GPS distance.
  - Calendar options: auto sync, look-ahead, all-day events, clear calendar quests.

- **Map:** shrines are tall beacons with floating crystals and beams of light;
  monsters stand on the map; villain camps fly banners; your hero stands in a

- **Candy:** taming gives 3 candy for that monster's line and releasing gives
  more; spend candy to level monsters up. Stat points never change with level.
- **Storage:** 20 monster slots to start; level upgrades grow the bag and
  monster storage together; buy +50 for 1,000 gold.
- **15 new monsters:** slime, rock golem, thunderbird, shell turtle and imp lines.
- **Mythic art style:** ink outlines, engraving, rune circles, mist and glowing
  eyes; evolutions gain auras, spirit wings, halos and crowns.
- **Journal, armor, gear on the hero, Pokémon GO-style spawns and lures,
  Adventurer class, quests dashboard,** thugs and villains, weapon types and
  rarities, and walking rewards.
