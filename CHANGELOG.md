# Changelog

## 1.32.0

- **Dark map:** Settings > Appearance > Map: Match app (dark with the dark
  theme), Light or Dark. The dark map keeps the map's own colors (parks
  green, water blue), just dimmed, and never flashes white while loading.
- **Cleaner text everywhere:** small text no longer gets the large default
  line spacing, so descriptions and notes stop looking double-spaced on
  every screen.
- **Consistent headings:** the Health card and the Swap slots use the same
  heading styles as the rest of the game.
- **Map footer:** what's around (monsters near, tracked, shrines, dens,
  foes) shows as small pills that wrap neatly instead of one long line.

## 1.31.0

- **Life Absorb (new Corrupt attack):** dark power, not just dirty tricks.
  80 power, and the user heals 25% of the damage it deals. Corrupt monsters
  learn it at level 48.

## 1.30.0

- **Health card on Home:** a card at the top of the Home screen. It folds
  to one line (today's steps and water) and opens with a tap; it remembers
  whether you left it open.
  - **Steps by hour:** today's steps, progress toward 5,000, and a bar for
    every hour. Tap a bar for that hour's count; the busiest hour is
    named. With Health Connect as the step source, the hours come straight
    from Health Connect (including before you opened the game); otherwise
    the game counts them as steps come in.
  - **Water tracker:** + and − a glass at a time (8 oz, or 250 ml with
    metric units), against a daily goal you can set from 4 to 16 glasses.
    It's just for you: no rewards.
  - **Sleep (coming soon):** a preview of the sleep tracker with sample
    numbers, so you can see what it will look like. Nothing is tracked yet.

## 1.29.0

- **56 new attacks: 8 more for every type**, learned at levels 6, 14, 21,
  29, 36, 44, 52 and 60. Each type now fights its own way:
  - **Fire, raw damage:** the hardest hitters, often with a cost: Cinder
    Jab, Flare Rush, Searing Blade, Magma Fist, Flare Blitz (120, always
    recoil), Meteor Flame, Sunfire, Supernova (150 to every foe, then rest).
  - **Electric, status:** shocks, and new *status bonus* moves that hit
    harder when the foe already has a status: Static Jolt, Galvanize (double
    power), Charge Net, Ion Storm, Volt Snare, Plasma Lance, Thunderclap,
    Judgment Bolt.
  - **Water, balanced and fast:** new *Swift* moves let the user go first
    next round: Ripple Dart, Rapid Current, Surf, Riptide, Undertow, Torrent
    Rush, Tidal Wave, Tsunami.
  - **Flight, balanced with self boosts:** Wing Flick, Sky Uppercut,
    Updraft, Air Slash, Soaring Strike, Ascent, Hurricane, Talon Storm.
  - **Gas, status:** poison, sleep and status bonuses: Noxious Spit,
    Venoshock, Drowsy Mist, Toxic Cloud, Sludge Bomb, Plague Fang, Caustic
    Burst, Pandemic.
  - **Corrupt, dirty fighting:** attacks that drop the foe's stats: Low
    Blow, Spiteful Jab, Eye Gouge, Backstab, Rend, Dread Howl, Hollowing
    Strike, Doomfall.
  - **Earth, built on Defense:** some damage, plenty of Defense, and new
    *Defense strike* moves that hit with the user's Defense instead of its
    Attack: Stone Knuckle, Bedrock Press, Mud Slap, Shield Bash, Rockslide,
    Iron Root, Mountain Crush, Titan Slam.
- Monsters already past those levels know the new moves right away.
  Automatic loadouts pick them up; custom loadouts can swap them in on the
  Monsters tab.

## 1.28.0

- **Hold a move to see what it does:** in battle, press and hold any move
  button for its type, power, accuracy (or "Never misses"), how it does
  against the current foe, and its side effects.
- **Scare and Intimidate split up:** they used to do the same thing.
  Intimidate is now only a skill (dragons and some heroes): every foe's
  Attack drops to 67% as the battle starts, with extra drops at ranks 3
  and 5. Scare is now a Normal attack (power 50, learned at level 9) that
  hits and drops the foe's Attack to 67%. The two don't stack. Brute thugs
  now have the Intimidate skill.
- **Moves reworked so none overlap:** within each type, no two attacks do
  the same job any more.
  - Normal: Bite 50; Quick Strike 35 (never misses); Slam became Body Slam
    (75, 30% chance to lower Defense).
  - Fire: Fire Fang 55 (burn and flinch chance); Fire Storm 90 and hits
    every foe.
  - Electric: Shock 25; Thunder Shock 55 (20% shock); Thunder Wave hits
    every foe.
  - Water: Water Shot pierces guards; Tidal Slam 80 with flinch; Hydro Blast
    120 at 80% accuracy; Hydro Surprise 90 with a high critical-hit chance.
  - Flight: Wind Tunnel hits every foe; Flying Kick 95 with recoil.
  - Gas: Puff can make the foe miss; Gunk Shot 85 at 80% accuracy with a 30%
    poison chance (now learned at 25); Toxic Wave hits every foe.
  - Corrupt: Dark Wave hits every foe; Shadow Claw pierces guards.
  - Earth: Sap heals half the damage dealt; Rock Throw and Vine Whip lower
    Defense and Speed; Earthquake 90 and hits every foe.

## 1.27.0

- **Moves fit on screen:** on phones the battle log is smaller (compact
  lines), and your team and moves get more of the screen, so the four moves
  and the Items / Tame / Swap / Flee row fit without scrolling.
- **Swap after a faint:** when one of your monsters faints and someone is
  waiting in a swap slot, the game asks whether to send them in. It's free:
  it doesn't use anyone's turn. Say "Not now" and, when the fainted
  monster's turn comes round, it gets two choices: Swap or Skip.

- **Softer color:** both widgets use the wallpaper's gentle neutral tone,
  slightly less colorful than before.
- **Tall widgets show the list:** taller-than-wide sizes (like 2x3 or 2x4)

## 1.26.1

## 1.26.0

- **Gear tiers by level:** better materials now unlock at milestones
  instead of creeping up slowly toward level 100.

  | From level | What you find |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | Mostly wood and leather |
  | 10 | Bronze turns up often |
  | 20 | Bronze is usual; iron appears |
  | 30 | Iron is as common as bronze |
  | 40 | Iron is usual; gold appears |
  | 50 | Gold turns up often |
  | 65 | Gold is usual |
  | 80 | Mostly gold, with the best diamond odds |

## 1.24.0

## 1.23.0

- **Evolving is your choice:** monsters no longer evolve on their own when
  they level up. When one reaches its evolution level it shows "Ready to
  evolve!" (and the level-up pop-up says so), and an **Evolve** button
  appears on its page. Evolving costs candy of its line: 10 for the first
  evolution, 25 for the second and 50 for the third. Wild monsters still
  appear already evolved for their level.
- **Candy for beating monsters:** every monster you defeat gives 1 candy of
  its line (3 for bosses and den monsters), on top of the 3 for taming one.

## 1.22.0

- **The map follows you** like Pokemon GO: it stays centered on your hero
  and moves as you walk. Dragging no longer slides it around.
- **Zooming:** pinch, double-tap to zoom in, or double-tap and hold, then
  slide down to zoom in or up to zoom out. Zoom always stays centered on
  you, from neighbourhood view down to street level.
- Tapping something in the Nearby list still shows where it is; the map
  returns to you after 10 seconds, or straight away with "Back to me".

## 1.21.0

- **Materials:** weapons come in wooden, bronze, iron, golden and diamond;
  armor in leather, bronze, iron, gold and diamond. Each better material
  adds more to every bonus. Early on you mostly find wood and leather with
  some bronze; better materials get likelier as you level, but capped: a
  level 10 hero can find diamond (about 1 in 800), and even at level 100
  it stays about 1 in 20.
- **Armor scrap:** thugs and villains now drop armor scrap as well as weapon
  parts. Five of one kind forge that armor, and armor can be smelted into
  scrap like weapons. Scrap stays in the Bag.
- **Equipment dropdowns:** on the Hero tab, tap any slot to see the piece's
  full stats and effects and swap in anything from your bag that fits.
  Slots now run top to toe: weapon, head, body, back, legs, feet.
- **Weekend Gold shrine rewards:** beating a weekend guardian pays gold and
  spell pages, with a 75% chance of a spell book (a whole spell learned at
  once, or a rank up if you know them all) and a 40% chance of cursed
  Legendary gear.
- **Cursed gear:** Frost (gives frostbite: 5% HP a turn, none in a drought),
  Life Drain (a weapon heals you 30% of its damage; armor steals 10% of a
  physical hit back) or Flame (burns). Until the curse is lifted the gear
  drains 3% of your HP each turn and a cursed weapon hits 25% softer. Every
  hit lifts 2% of the curse (a curse bar shows under the piece on the Hero
  tab). Once lifted it stays Legendary, keeps its power and gains two new
  effects.
- **This week** now sits above the calendar on the Quests screen.
- **App icon:** the crest is sized to fit launcher icon shapes, so the
  whole ring shows, matching the loading screen.
- **Home-screen widget:** today's reminders, upcoming calendar quests and
  your to-do list, in Material 3 colors (your wallpaper's on Android 12+).
  Resize it freely: small shows the next few things, bigger shows each
  section. Tap it to open the game.

## 1.20.0

- **Daily reminders:** gentle alarms set in the game, at a time of day on
  the days you pick ("6:00 AM, make the bed, every day but Saturday").
  Add them with "+ Reminder" on the Quests screen.
  - At its time you get one notification with **Done**, **Skip** and
    **Snooze 15 min** buttons, so you never have to open the game. Taps
    are added to your save the next time the game is open.
  - **On-time rewards:** marking one done within 3 hours of its time pays
    like an Easy quest (+15 XP, +8 gold) and shares the daily quest
    allowance. Ticking it early or late pays nothing.
  - **Streaks:** each reminder keeps a streak over the days it rings. 7 days
    in a row gives a Monster Treat; every 30 days, a Restore Potion and
    150 gold. Skipping a busy day doesn't break the streak.
  - **No penalty:** missing one costs nothing; its streak just starts over.
  - Alarms come back after the phone restarts or the time zone changes.

## 1.19.1

- **Tasks first:** on the Quests screen, your Tasks (things without a due
  date) now sit above the scheduled and calendar quests.

## 1.19.0

- **Orbs locked by level:** Taming Orbs for everyone, Great Orbs from level
  10, the new Mega Orbs (160 gold) from 25 and Ultra Orbs (320 gold) from
  50. The shop shows a lock with the level until then.
- **Perfect Orb** (level 75, 2,000 gold): always tames, even a monster at
  full health (but not a boss you haven't beaten yet). It's never found
  in the wild; reaching level 100 gives you one.
- **Level-up boxes:** every level now gives a small box of 2 to 4 items
  that gets better as you level: orbs that follow the shop's level locks,
  Potions then Hi-Potions, plus an Ether, Phoenix Down or Restore Potion
  every 5 levels and a Monster Treat every 10.
- **Full bag, no punishment:** a level-up box always arrives in full, even
  if it overfills your bag. You keep everything, but you can't pick up or
  buy anything else until you've used, sold or dropped enough to make room.
  The bag shows how many items to clear.

## 1.18.0

- **Fair quest rewards** (no farming with fake quests):
  - A new task pays out only once it's been around for 15 minutes; it
    shows "Reward unlocks in N min" until then. Calendar quests pay once
    they've started. Ticking one early still marks it done, with no
    reward.
  - The first 8 quests each day pay in full, the next 4 pay half, and
    after that quests still count for your streak but pay nothing. The
    Quests screen shows how many full rewards are left today.
  - Only 3 Hard quests a day pay the Hard rate; the rest pay as Normal.
  - Doing the same quest (same name) again on the same day pays half.
  - The daily counts are kept on your hero, so deleting and re-adding
    quests doesn't reset them. They reset at midnight.

## 1.16.0

- **New spell: Mud Shot** (Earth magic). It does little damage (power 12,
  +4 per rank) for 3 Energy, but each hit lowers the foe's accuracy by 10%
  for the rest of the battle. It stacks up to 5 times (−50%), and the foe
  shows "Acc ↓" in battle. Learn it with 5 spell-book pages from item
  shrines, then rank it up to 5 like the other spells.

## 1.15.3

- **Sort by potential:** a new "Potential" sort option next to "Newest"

## 1.15.2

- **Drag to scroll:** while holding a monster to move it into the party,

## 1.15.1

- **Class dropdown:** the Hero tab shows just your class. Tap it ("Change
  ▾") to open the other classes and switch; tap again to close. Your

## 1.15.0

- **Energy carries over:** your Energy no longer refills after each
  battle. It comes back slowly over time (all of it in 6 hours), faster by
  walking (2 for every 100 steps), or all at once with an Ether, which now
  works from the bag too. The Hero tab shows an Energy bar.
- **Thugs look evil, and each kind is different:**
  - **Brute** (warrior): horned iron helm, spiked shoulders, a spiked club.
    Tough and hard-hitting, but slow.
  - **Hexer** (mage): a tall crooked hood with nothing inside but glowing
    eyes, a rune-scrawled robe and a skull staff. Casts Corrupt magic, but
    is frail.
  - **Cutthroat** (rogue): masked, with a blood-red sash and twin curved
    daggers. Fast, dodges, and fights dirty.

  All of them have glowing red eyes.

## 1.14.2

- **Quest buttons:** "+ To-do" is gone. Things with a date go on your
  calendar (the Calendar button opens it) and come into the game from
  there. "+ Task" stays for things without a due date.
- **Sync:** a new button pulls in your calendar right away, in case the
  game has fallen behind. Without calendar access, it asks for it.

## 1.14.1

- **Smack Down:** a new Earth move (power 65) that can hit fliers. Against
  a hovering foe it hits at 85 and knocks it down, so Earth moves hit it
  for the rest of the battle. Earth monsters learn it at level 20 and most
  others at 24. Fish, slimes, wisps, the Prism Fairy and the Sky Serpent
  can't learn it.
- **Hover, balanced:** at rank 3 the team takes 25% less Earth damage, and
  50% at rank 4. At rank 5 the whole team hovers, but only until the hero
  takes a hit. Then the team drops back to 50% (the hero keeps floating).
  Fighters show "Hovering" or "Grounded" in battle.

## 1.14.0

- **Battery saver draws more trouble:** with the phone upside down in the
  battery saver, surprise attacks while walking go from a 25% to a 40%
  chance every 50 steps, and aggressive monsters notice you from twice as
  far. An attack buzzes, wakes the screen and starts the fight.
- **Thugs:** about 1 in 5 surprise attacks (from level 3) is a thug who
  jumps you with one monster for each monster you have fighting. The thug
  fights alongside them, so it's an even match. Their monsters can't be
  tamed, and the pay grows with the size of the crew.
- **Swap slots:** two more slots under your battle party. Monsters there
  come along to every fight and can be sent in with the new **Swap**
  button, which replaces Guard (the hero keeps Defend). Use it when a
  monster faints, or to swap a tired one out. Swap monsters get half XP
  unless they actually fight.
- **A guard move for every type:** only Water had one (Wave Guard). Now
  every type learns its own at level 12: Flame Shield, Static Barrier,
  Wind Wall, Toxic Veil, Shadow Veil and Stone Wall.
- **No playing at driving speed:** above 15 mph, monsters stop appearing
  and GPS distance stops counting as walking. A pop-up asks you not to
  play while driving. Monsters come back after a minute at walking pace
  (or a few minutes after stopping).
- Added a **move store** idea to the to-do list.

## 1.13.0

- **Weapon class bonus:** a weapon of your class adds +2, +4, +6 or +8
  (Normal, Rare, Epic, Legendary) to Attack, Magic, Defense and Speed, on
  top of the +20% power it already had. A weapon outside your class takes
  the same amount off. The penalty fades as you fight with that weapon and
  is gone after 10 battles. Practice counts for that one weapon only: a
  Warrior who masters one staff starts over with the next.
- **Subclasses at level 15:** three for each class.

  | Class | Subclasses |
  |---|---|
  | Warrior | Berserker (more Attack, stronger as HP drops), Paladin (Defense, better healing, starts behind a ward), Warlord (your monsters +8%) |
  | Mage | Elementalist (+25% spell damage), Cleric (+50% healing and wards), Warlock (spells drain life) |
  | Rogue | Assassin (more and bigger critical hits), Trickster (dodge, feints that throw foes off), Beastmaster (taming odds, monsters +5%) |
  | Adventurer | Jack of All Trades (every weapon counts as yours), Survivor (more HP, heals every turn), Wayfarer (+20% gold and XP) |

  The first pick is free; switching costs 300 gold.
- **Hero skills:** a skill slot opens at levels 30, 45 and 60. Skills rank
  up every 10 levels (up to rank 5), and from rank 3 some cover your whole
  team. Pick from Morale Master, Kleptomaniac (carry a second weapon),
  Insomnia, Intimidate, Hover, Oblivious, Quick Step, Treasure Hunter,
  Scholar, Second Wind, First Strike, Beast Whisperer, Regeneration, Eagle
  Eye and Mana Well. Swapping a skill costs 500 gold.
- **Monster skills at level 30:** every monster gets one that fits what it
  is. Birds, bats, wisps and fairies levitate (Earth moves miss them; Earth
  monsters never do), dragons intimidate, hounds lead their pack, slimes
  and trees regenerate, and shells and bears have thick hides. Wild
  monsters have them too.
- **Selling:** the shop now buys items at half price, and weapons and armor
  by rarity and level. Smelt a weapon for 50 gold to get 1 to 4 parts of
  its type back, depending on rarity.

## 1.12.1

## 1.12.0

- **New type chart:** Electric now beats Flight, and Flight beats Earth. It's
  still perfectly balanced, like rock-paper-scissors with seven throws: every
  type is strong against two, weak to two and neutral to two.

  | Type | Strong against | Weak to |
  |---|---|---|
  | Water | Fire, Gas | Earth, Electric |
  | Fire | Earth, Electric | Water, Corrupt |
  | Corrupt | Fire, Flight | Earth, Gas |
  | Gas | Corrupt, Electric | Water, Flight |
  | Earth | Water, Corrupt | Fire, Flight |
  | Flight | Earth, Gas | Electric, Corrupt |
  | Electric | Water, Flight | Fire, Gas |

  The Type chart on the Monsters tab and the Help screen show the new chart.

## 1.11.1

- **Stronger mythics:** Prism Fairy and Mimic are now as strong as a final
  evolution (stats x1.45, stronger signature attacks), still below the
  starters and legendaries. To match, they're rarer: Epic rarity (harder to
  find and to tame) and found from level 15. Ones you already own get the
  boost too.

## 1.11.0

- **Tasks:** things without a due date now have their own **Tasks** section
  at the end of the Quests list (shown only when you have some), separate
  from the **Scheduled** quests grouped by when they're due. Add one with the
  new **+ Task** button; tap a task to give it a due date later. **+ To-do**
  now starts with a due time this evening, which you can change.
- **Starter signature moves:** each starter line learns two strong moves no
  other monster has, at level 22 and level 40:
  - Voltfawn line: **Storm Antlers** (hits every foe, 30% shock each) and
    **Heaven's Charge** (130 power, pierces guards and raised Defense, raises
    Speed).
  - Cindrake line: **Wyrmfire** (always burns, plus 3 turns of scorching) and
    **Inferno Wings** (hits every foe, then raises Attack).
  - Drizzlet line: **Tidal Embrace** (heals half the damage it deals) and
    **Maelstrom Vortex** (115 power, brings rain, traps the foe for 4 turns).
  - Mosscub line: **Grove Guardian** (heals the whole team 20% and raises
    everyone's Defense) and **Tectonic Fury** (hits every foe, 30% chance
    each loses its turn).

  (follows your phone's light/dark setting, the default), Light or Dark. The
  light theme is warm parchment with deeper gold, green and blue accents so
  everything stays readable; status bar icons switch to match.
- **Match system colors (Material You):** Settings, then Appearance, then
  Colors. "Monster Quest" keeps the game's gold-and-teal look; "Match system

## 1.8.1

- **Your battle party stays with you:** monsters in your battle party can't
  be sent on adventures; only bench monsters are offered. And monsters out
  on an adventure can't be dragged into the party until they're back.

## 1.8.0

- **Surprise attacks while walking:** with the map open, every 50 steps
  there's a 25% chance a monster leaps out in front of you. The phone gives a
  long buzz, an **AMBUSHED!** pop-up appears over the map, and the battle
  starts by itself (or tap Fight!) with the monsters striking first.
  - Usually one monster; sometimes 2 or 3 of the same kind, and then each is
    proportionally weaker (a trio has less total HP than one alone).
  - Never during a battle or phone-free session, not within a minute of your

## 1.7.1

- **Many more monsters around you.** Monsters now also wander close by (from
  about 230 ft out), not only at the far edge of your range, more spawn
  overall, and they're more curious, drifting toward you faster even when you
  stand still. On average:
  - Standing still: about 4 on the map (was under 1), 1–2 close enough to

  - Strolling: about 9 on the map (was 3–4).
  - Walking briskly: about 18 on the map, plus the shrine monsters.

## 1.7.0

- **Quests on big screens:** unfolded phones and tablets show the Quests tab

  quest list on the right (split at the hinge on a book-style fold).
- **Tap a day on the calendar** to see that day's quests, like a calendar

  day again (or Show all) to go back to every quest. On phones the list
  scrolls into view.
- Days with quests due get a gold dot, and the calendar now goes forward
  into future months too.

## 1.6.0

- **Due dates:** give a to-do a due date and time: quick picks (in 1 hour,
  this evening, tomorrow 9 AM), any date and time, or none. Calendar quests

- **Priority:** Low, Normal or High, separate from difficulty (which still
